# Genetics extraction pilot — 2026-10-30

Sources: [Jinek et al. 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22745249/) and [Cong et al. 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23287718/). The repeatable live runner is `src/modules/12-evaluation/genetic-pilot.live.test.ts` (opt-in via `LIVE_GENETIC_EVAL=1`).

Two live abstract-only extraction runs returned 3+5 and 4+3 proposed findings, respectively. The full extraction-and-verification run returned four supported claims from each abstract, with zero technical drops. At least one Cong claim merely restated background or the fact that two systems were engineered rather than an editing capability. The support judge accepted that claim because support against the quoted source and relevance to the question are separate checks.

These numbers are **not precision or recall**: no independent reviewer has yet annotated all findings, matched proposals to gold findings, or checked the long article. `src/modules/12-evaluation/genetic-extraction.ts` now computes both metrics from explicit reviewer mappings and records drop reasons. It also computes a four-class support-judge confusion matrix and Cohen's kappa from human labels. A reviewer should annotate each gold finding, then mark every proposed claim as relevant/irrelevant and link it to a gold id. Keep a held-out article separate from prompt tuning.

The first long-article attempt exposed a retrieval failure: Europe PMC's `fullTextXML` returned HTTP 500 for Cong despite a PMC record. The NCBI BioC fallback returned sectioned full text, about 9,922 characters. Before section separation, the long article produced six claims in one extraction batch. After section separation it produced ten proposed claims across three passages and two concurrent extraction batches, including specific EMX1, Th and PVALB results absent from the abstract. Some overlap with abstract findings remains. This is a coverage improvement, **not yet a measured recall improvement**; duplicate and relevance review is required before display.

The mutation suite currently catches changed quotations, numeric values in claim text and structured conditions, and a quote attached to a different source. Negation, scope, and strength changes require live support-judge trials against reviewer-labelled cases. Those target rates remain unmeasured.

The first full long-article verification returned eight supported claims and two numeric drops. Inspection showed both drops were validator errors: the digit in `SpCas9` was read as a measurement, and `1.6 %` did not match `1.6%`. After fixing numeric token boundaries and unit spacing, a repeated live verification returned ten supported claims and zero technical drops. The source-support judge also accepted a low-relevance statement that the authors engineered two systems. Thus the current verified count is **not** a precision estimate; question relevance needs independent review and a stricter extraction criterion.

P5@0.7 now explicitly excludes setup and background statements that do not answer the user's question. In a live abstract rerun it returned four Jinek and three Cong findings, omitting the previously repeated Cong setup statement. A live long-article rerun returned three abstract and six body proposals across the two section batches. This is a useful qualitative regression check, not a precision or recall estimate; one body proposal widened its scope to "across organisms" and still needs verification and reviewer assessment.

Three live semantic mutation probes used a Jinek passage: negation was judged `no` with a `negation` issue; an unsupported human-cell scope was judged `partial` with `wrong_scope`; the strength escalation produced an invalid judge response after retry, so the product would conservatively keep it unconfirmed. This is 2/3 usable verdicts in a tiny diagnostic sample, **not** the planned ≥90% mutation detection rate or a human calibration. A 60-claim independently labelled sample is still required for Cohen's κ and an error matrix.

The existing concordance pass is the adversarial mechanism for this product: it evaluates relationships among already verified, source-grounded claims after the initial answer. It must not search for or invent opposition to satisfy a comparison request. A separate open-ended adversary agent is deferred unless labelled divergence cases show that concordance systematically misses real counterevidence.
